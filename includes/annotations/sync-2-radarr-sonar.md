[^1]:

    - <ins>**Announced**</ins>: Radarr shall consider movies available as soon as they are added to Radarr. This setting is recommended if you have good private trackers that do not have fakes.
    - <ins>**In Cinemas**</ins>: Radarr shall consider movies available as soon as movies hit cinemas (Theatrical Date on TMDb) This option is not recommended.
    - <ins>**Released**</ins>: Radarr shall consider movies available as soon as the Blu-Ray or streaming version is released (Digital and Physical dates on TMDb) This option is recommended and likely should be combined with an Availability Delay of `-14` or `-21` days.

[^2]:

    - <ins>**All Episodes**</ins>: Monitor all episodes except specials
    - <ins>**Future Episodes**</ins>: Monitor episodes that have not aired yet
    - <ins>**Missing Episodes**</ins>: Monitor episodes that do not have files or have not aired yet
    - <ins>**Existing Episodes**</ins>: Monitor episodes that have files or have not aired yet
    - <ins>**Recent Episodes**</ins>: Monitor episodes aired within the last 90 days and future episodes
    - <ins>**Pilot Episode**</ins>: Only monitor the first episode of the first season
    - <ins>**First Season**</ins>: Monitor all episodes of the first season. All other seasons will be ignored
    - <ins>**Last Season**</ins>: Monitor all episodes of the last season
    - <ins>**Monitor Specials**</ins>: Monitor all special episodes without changing the monitored status of other episodes
    - <ins>**Unmonitor Specials**</ins>: Unmonitor all special episodes without changing the monitored status of other episodes
    - <ins>**None**</ins>: No episodes will be monitored

[^3]:

    - <ins>**All Seasons**</ins>: Monitor all new seasons automatically
    - <ins>**No New Seasons**</ins>: Do not monitor any new seasons automatically

[^4]:

    - <ins>**Anime**</ins>: Episodes released using an absolute episode number
    - <ins>**Daily**</ins>: Episodes released daily or less frequently that use year-month-day (2017-05-25)
    - <ins>**Standard**</ins>: Episodes released with SxxEyy pattern
